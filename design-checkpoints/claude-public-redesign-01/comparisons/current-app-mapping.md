## Summary

The archive is strongest as a compact desktop visual direction: bounded scenic
composition, dense cards, crisp sprite stages, and restrained charcoal/gold
hierarchy. The riskiest areas are the duplicated shell/Appearance boundary,
invented gameplay relationships and fields, client-only filtering/infinite
loading, and absent responsive/accessibility behavior. Those require fresh
implementation against the current contracts, not direct source reuse.
